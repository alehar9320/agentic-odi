Act as a principal ux designer like Steve Jobs, that also happens to be AI native and on top of conversational agentic experiences best practice. Evaluate the experience and identify key improvements that will help abstract away the complexities of terminology like "jobs to be done", or "ODI guidelines", into rather this being your agent that interprets your intent and needs, and translates that into these structures beneath the surface. As such, you don't ask the user technical terms, but rather "what is your organization currently lookin to achieve?", or "what has your boss asked you to drive forward?", "can you tell me about yourself, so that I know how to best help you?", or "don't know what to build or why? let me help you brainstorm" (with suggestions from the likes of YCombinator, or famous examples, that we yet expect may have gaps in the market). Alternatively, "what is the key priority for the business right now, related to your domain?". When these questions are answered, the conversation tracks and stores data behind-the-scenes. Both full transcripts, but also ultimately slotting it into relevant data structures that the user can either just let sit, or manually interact with at a later occasion.
